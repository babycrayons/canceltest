The Cancel Test

100 things the internet might never let you live down. Check every one that applies to you, then see how fast the mob would find you. Satire. Not a moral ranking, and your answers stay on your device.

Made for fun. The politics items are deliberately two-sided: both “support” and “don’t support” count, because someone online will be mad either way.
